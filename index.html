I am a Master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, working on generative AI and controllable face generation under the supervision of Dr. Xianxu Hou.

My current research reformulates kinship face synthesis as identity-aware diffusion on top of frozen personalised diffusion backbones — with the goal of generating child faces that are faithful to their parents while maintain fidelity.

Before AI, I earned a B.Eng. in Civil Engineering and spent two years as an infrastructure engineer at Hangzhou Red Cross Hospital, where I built Stable Diffusion + ControlNet workflows for renovation design and ESP32-CAM edge-vision pipelines.

Publications

InheriDiffusion: Inheritance-Aware Diffusion Models for Kinship Face Synthesis
Matao Ye, Xianxu Hou, et al.
CVPR 2026 Workshop (HiGen) Accepted

We keep a pretrained personalised diffusion backbone frozen and learn inheritance directly inside its identity-embedding space. A lightweight Identity Inheritance Encoder (bidirectional cross-attention + nonlinear blending) and a Parental Guidance Attention module re-inject parental embeddings throughout denoising to restore fine-grained hereditary detail without any backbone fine-tuning. Substantially improves hereditary consistency over prior methods on TSKinFace, FF-Database and FIW.
